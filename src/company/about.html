Most hemp is sold on vibes. A vague strength on the front, no certificate, and a customer left to work out the rest by trial and error. That is a bad way to sell something people put in their bodies.

What we actually do differently

Every batch is third party tested before it ships, and the certificate is published rather than filed. Onset and duration are printed on the package in plain language, not buried in a FAQ. And the number on the front of the pack is the number the lab found, within 5%.

Why the mountain

A sherpa does not carry you up. They know the route, they tell you what the next section is going to feel like, and they make sure you are equipped for it. That is the entire product philosophy, and it is why the dose instrument on the homepage is the first thing you meet rather than a product grid.

Texas

Grown and crafted in San Antonio. Every Sherpa product is hemp-derived Delta-9 THC at or below 0.3% by dry weight, the standard under both Texas and federal law. Nothing in the lineup contains Delta-8 or Delta-10, which is why the state's ban on those isomers took nothing off our shelf.
