## Dosing

### How much should I take the first time?

Start at 5 mg and wait a full two hours before deciding it did not work. That is the single most common mistake: taking a second dose at 45 minutes, then having both land at once. The dose instrument on the homepage walks through what each step actually feels like.

### Why does onset vary so much between products?

How it gets into you. A nano-emulsified drink disperses in water and lands in about 15 minutes. An edible has to go through your digestive system first, which takes 45 to 90 minutes and varies with what else you have eaten.

### What happens if I take too much?

You will be uncomfortable, not in danger. Sit down somewhere familiar, drink water, and wait it out. It passes. [PLACEHOLDER] A support number should be listed here.

### Can I mix formats?

Add the milligrams together and treat it as one dose. A 10 mg seltzer and a 10 mg gummy is a 20 mg evening, with two different onset times stacked on top of each other, which is harder to judge than a single 20 mg dose.

## Products

### What is the difference between Delta-8, Delta-10 and Delta-9?

They are different THC isomers. Sherpa only makes hemp-derived Delta-9 THC at or below 0.3% by dry weight. Nothing in the range contains Delta-8 or Delta-10, which is why the Texas ban on those two does not affect any Sherpa product.

### Is Dawg Snax going to get my dog high?

No. Dawg Snax is CBD only and contains no THC at all.

### What is nano-emulsification?

Breaking the THC into droplets small enough to disperse in water rather than sitting in oil. It is the reason the seltzers and Quick Stix land faster and more predictably than an edible.

### Do the gummies taste like cannabis?

They are built on real fruit purée rather than a distillate carrier, which is where that taste normally comes from.
