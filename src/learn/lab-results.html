What a certificate
actually tells you.

Two things matter on a COA and most brands bury both. The first is whether the potency found matches the potency claimed. The second is whether the safety screens passed.

Claim against found

A 10 mg can should test near 10 mg. Ours run within 5%. A brand that will not print the found number is telling you something.

Four safety screens

Pesticides, heavy metals, residual solvents, microbials. All four have to pass, on every batch, not on a representative sample.

Batch and date

A certificate with no batch number cannot be matched to the thing in your hand. Ours is printed on the box as a QR code.
